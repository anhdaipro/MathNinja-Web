Account & Data Deletion Request

Last Updated: April 10, 2026

At Math Ninja, we respect your privacy and uphold your complete right to data sovereignty. You can request the permanent and irreversible deletion of your profile and all associated cloud-stored game progress at any time.

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant)

You can immediately wipe all player data directly inside the mobile app without waiting:

  1. Launch the Math Ninja app on your iOS or Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings icon located on the Home Screen.
  3. Scroll down to the Data & Privacy section and select Delete Account / Reset Data.
  4. Confirm your choice. Your cloud player record, avatar, and high score entries will be permanently purged immediately.

Method 2: Submit a Deletion Request via Email

If you have uninstalled the app or lost access to your device, our support team can manually process your deletion request:

  • Support Email: daip6690@gmail.com
  • Subject: Math Ninja Account Deletion Request
  • Information required: Please mention your Player Display Name, approximate registration date, or Device ID if known.

Notice: Manual requests are verified and permanently purged from our Supabase production clusters within 48 hours.

What Data Is Permanently Purged?

Upon deletion confirmation, the following records are permanently erased from our systems:

  • Player profile entry (Display Name, chosen Avatar, Country tag).
  • Leaderboard high score submissions across all game modes (Calculus Arena, Chrono Tower, Math Snake, Bubble Arena, Vector Ball).
  • FCM Device Push notification tokens.
  • Saved game statistics, streak counters, and unlocked blade achievements.
