Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 10, 2026

Thank you for playing Math Ninja ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to our mobile application and related web services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve your gaming experience:

  • Device & Profile Identifiers: Randomly generated unique device identifiers, country code, chosen player display name, and avatar to maintain leaderboard scores.
  • Game Progress & Analytics: High scores in Calculus Arena, Chrono Tower floors completed, streak counts, and in-game achievements.
  • Crash Logs & Performance: Diagnostic information, crash dumps, and frame performance data to maintain our 120 FPS target engine.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely for the following purposes:

  • To sync your game progress across devices using Supabase cloud infrastructure.
  • To display your global ranking on public leaderboards.
  • To deliver game reminders, streak freezes, and update notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
  • To serve non-intrusive advertisements supporting our free-to-play model.

3. Third-Party Services

We partner with trusted service providers who comply with strict data protection standards:

  • Supabase: Cloud database storage and profile authentication.
  • Google AdMob & SKAdNetwork: In-app advertising network.
  • Google Firebase: Cloud messaging (push notifications) and crash reporting.
  • Apple App Store & Google Play: In-app purchases and subscription processing.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Math Ninja is designed for general audiences and educational mathematics practice. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full authority over your personal data. You may request account and associated cloud data deletion at any time directly through the app or by visiting our Account Deletion Page.
